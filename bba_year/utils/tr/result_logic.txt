# BBA / BCA Hons Result Calculation Rules

This document explains exactly how the system decides a student's result.

## 1. Passing a SINGLE Subject
A student MUST pass both the Theory (ESE) and Internal (CIA) parts for every subject.
- Honours Paper: Must score at least 45% in both parts.
- Subsidiary Paper: Must score at least 35% in both parts.
*If a student fails in even one part (Theory or Internal), they fail the entire subject.*

## 2. 1st Year and 2nd Year (Part I & II) Rules
The result is calculated based on the total aggregate marks:
- PASS WITH HONS. : Passed all subjects individually.
- PASS : Not all subjects passed, but Total Marks are >= Total Pass Threshold (680/1600 or approx 42.5%).
- FAIL : Total Marks are below the passing threshold.

## 3. 3rd Year (Final Year / Graduation) Rules
In the final year, the aggregate of all 3 years is checked:
- 1st/2nd Class : Every subject from 1st, 2nd, and 3rd year is passed individually.
- PASS : Not all subjects passed, but the overall 3-year aggregate is >= Total Pass Threshold.
- FAIL : Overall aggregate is below the threshold.

## 4. Degree Classification (Honours Division)
For graduating students, the final "Class" is decided by the total marks in Honours papers only:
- 1st Class With Distinction : 75% or above
- 1st Class : 60% to 74%
- 2nd Class : 45% to 59%

## 5. Important Notes
- ABSENT : Counts as 0 marks. If absent in any part, the student fails that subject.
- BACK PAPERS : The system automatically picks the best marks from all attempts.
- REMARKS : This column is always kept empty in the TR.
