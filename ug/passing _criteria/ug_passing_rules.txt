==================================================
RESULT LEVEL DEFINITIONS
==================================================

I. Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA)
   - Calculated at the END of EACH semester.

II. Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)
   - Calculated at the END of the PROGRAMME.

==================================================

==================================================

--------------------------------------------------
GRADE APPLICATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------

1. Grade is assigned based on FINAL MARKS of the course.
2. FAILED course:
   - Letter Grade = F
   - Numerical Grade = 0
   - Credit Earned = 0
3. ABSENT course:
   - Letter Grade = Ab
   - Numerical Grade = 0
   - Credit Earned = 0

==================================================
COURSE CREDIT RULE
==================================================

- Each course/paper has a FIXED credit value.
- Credit value is defined by the prescribed course structure.
- Credit value is NOT influenced by marks or grades.

COURSE CREDIT EARNING:
- PASSED course → FULL credit earned
- FAILED / ABSENT course → 0 credit
- Partial credit is NOT permitted.

==================================================
SEMESTER RESULT (PASS / PROMOTED / FAILED)
==================================================

If a student passes in:
- ALL CIA and ALL THEORY papers → PASS

If a student passes in:
- ALL CIA but FAILS in ANY THEORY paper → PROMOTED

If a student passes in:
- ALL THEORY but FAILS in ANY CIA → FAILED

--------------------------------------------------
GRACE RULE
--------------------------------------------------

- If a student FAILS in any semester:
  - Grace of MAXIMUM 05 marks (in split manner) may be provided
  - Grace is only to CLEAR failed subject(s)
  - Grace must NOT be used to improve grade or SGPA

==================================================
SEMESTER CREDIT & SGPA RULE
==================================================

semester_max_credit:
- Sum of credits prescribed for the semester.

semester_credit_earned:
- Sum of credits of PASSED courses only.

--------------------------------------------------
SGPA ELIGIBILITY (MANDATORY NOTE)
--------------------------------------------------

A candidate SHALL NOT be awarded or calculated
ANY SGPA if he/she FAILS to earn the TOTAL prescribed
credits in that particular semester.

--------------------------------------------------
SGPA FORMULA (AS PER ORDINANCE)
--------------------------------------------------

SGPA = ( Σ (m_i × o_i) ) / ( Σ o_i )

Where:
- n   = number of courses in the semester
- m_i = numerical grade obtained in course i
- o_i = credit of course i

FAILED / ABSENT courses:
- m_i = 0
- o_i = 0 (credit not earned)

==================================================
PROMOTION RULES
==================================================

--------------------------------------------------
PROMOTION TO SEMESTER III
--------------------------------------------------

ELIGIBLE if:
- Student passes in ALL CIA components, AND
- Secures AT LEAST 28 credits in Semester I & II taken together

NOT ELIGIBLE if:
- CIA passed but total credits in Sem I & II < 28

--------------------------------------------------
PROMOTION TO SEMESTER V
--------------------------------------------------

ELIGIBLE if:
- Student passes in ALL CIA components, AND
- Earns total credits of 40 (20 + 20) in Semester I & II, AND
- Earns AT LEAST 68 credits in Semester I, II, III & IV together

Else:
- NOT ELIGIBLE

==================================================
CGPA RULES (END OF PROGRAMME)
==================================================

- CGPA is calculated at the END of the programme.
- CGPA uses SGPA values of ALL semesters.

CGPA FORMULA:
CGPA = ( Σ (SGPA × Semester Credits) ) / ( Σ Semester Credits )

--------------------------------------------------
MINIMUM CGPA FOR DEGREE
--------------------------------------------------

- Minimum CGPA required for award of Bachelor Degree = 4.5

If:
- CGPA < 4.5
- BUT student earned REQUIRED credits
- AND passed in ALL papers/courses

Then:
- 0.5 shall be ADDED to actual CGPA
- Final CGPA SHALL NOT exceed 4.5

--------------------------------------------------
CGPA TO PERCENTAGE CONVERSION
--------------------------------------------------

% of Marks = CGPA × 10
